1. Who is responsible for your data

For anything concerning your data, write to WhatsApp +351 961 039 001.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer. We are not required to: we are a small operation and we do not carry out large-scale monitoring or large-scale processing of special categories of data.

2. What this notice covers

It covers this website and the contact that follows from it. It does not cover any other site you may reach from here — Google Maps and WhatsApp each have their own terms and their own privacy policies.

3. What the website itself collects: nothing

This is a static website. It has no accounts, no database and no server that receives anything you type. Everything you fill in stays inside your own browser.

In particular, the enquiry form on the contact section does not send anything to us. It assembles a message from the fields you complete, shows it to you, and then either copies it to your clipboard or opens WhatsApp with that message ready. Nothing leaves your device until you decide to send it, and when you do, you send it yourself, through WhatsApp.

5. Server logs

Like every website, this one is served by a hosting provider, and that provider's servers keep technical logs: the IP address of the request, the date and time, the page requested, and the browser's user-agent string. We do not use these logs to build profiles and we do not combine them with anything else.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in keeping the site available and secure. The hosting provider is GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), acting as our processor. Logs are kept for 90 days.

6. When you contact us on WhatsApp

If you send a message, the conversation happens on WhatsApp. WhatsApp is provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, which is a separate controller for the message data it handles — its own privacy policy applies to that, and we have no control over it. Messages on WhatsApp are end-to-end encrypted, but the fact that a conversation took place, and the metadata around it, is processed by Meta.

What we then hold is the conversation itself on the device used for the business account: your WhatsApp display name and number, the details you send about the occasion (date, city, type of occasion, duration, language), and anything else you choose to write.

Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into an arrangement, and performing it — Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Where an arrangement does not go ahead, our legitimate interest in keeping a short record of the exchange, Article 6(1)(f).

We keep conversations for 12 months after the last message and then delete them, unless we are required to keep something longer — Portuguese tax law, for instance, requires accounting records to be kept for ten years.

Please do not send us health details, identity documents, or anything about your sex life or beliefs. We do not need them and we do not want to hold them.

7. Who else sees your data

Nobody, beyond what is described above: the hosting provider that serves the pages, and Meta for the WhatsApp conversation. We do not sell data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not pass it to anyone else — unless we are legally obliged to, for example on a lawful order from a court or the authorities.

8. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Using WhatsApp may involve transfers of data to Meta companies outside the EEA. Those transfers happen under Meta's own safeguards, set out in its privacy policy. If you would rather not use WhatsApp, write to us on WhatsApp at the number in section 1.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us to give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you (access); correct it if it is wrong (rectification); delete it (erasure); limit what we do with it (restriction); hand it over in a portable format (portability); and you may object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interest. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time, without that affecting what was done before.

Write to WhatsApp +351 961 039 001. We will answer within one month. We may ask you something to confirm it is really you before we act.

If you believe we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Portuguese supervisory authority: Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), Av. D. Carlos I, 134 — 1.º, 1200-651 Lisboa, cnpd.pt. If you live in another EU country, you may complain to the authority there instead.

10. Adults only

This site is for people aged 18 or over and we do not knowingly process the data of anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has sent us information, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The device holding the business WhatsApp account is protected by a screen lock and the account by two-step verification. No system is perfect, but this one holds very little: most of what would be sensitive never reaches us in the first place.

12. Changes

If this notice changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will be flagged on the site.

Como qualquer site, este é servido por um alojamento, e os servidores desse alojamento guardam registos técnicos: o endereço IP do pedido, a data e a hora, a página pedida e a identificação do navegador. Não usamos estes registos para construir perfis nem os cruzamos com mais nada.

6. Quando nos contacta pelo WhatsApp

Se enviar mensagem, a conversa acontece no WhatsApp. O WhatsApp é fornecido pela Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, que é responsável autónoma pelos dados das mensagens que trata — a política de privacidade dela aplica-se a isso e nós não temos controlo sobre a mesma. As mensagens são cifradas ponta a ponta, mas o facto de ter havido conversa, e os metadados associados, são tratados pela Meta.

O que depois fica connosco é a própria conversa, no aparelho onde está a conta de negócio: o seu nome de utilizador e número de WhatsApp, os dados que enviar sobre a ocasião (data, cidade, tipo de ocasião, duração, idioma) e o mais que escolher escrever.

Fundamento: diligências pré-contratuais a seu pedido e execução do combinado — artigo 6.º, n.º 1, alínea b), do RGPD. Quando não se chega a combinar nada, o nosso interesse legítimo em guardar um registo breve da troca, alínea f).

Guardamos as conversas durante 12 meses após a última mensagem e depois apagamo-las, salvo se formos obrigados a conservar algo por mais tempo — a lei fiscal portuguesa, por exemplo, obriga a guardar documentos contabilísticos durante dez anos.

Por favor não nos envie dados de saúde, documentos de identificação, nem nada sobre a sua vida sexual ou as suas convicções. Não precisamos disso e não o queremos ter.

7. Quem mais vê os seus dados

Ninguém, além do descrito acima: o alojamento que serve as páginas e a Meta, no que toca à conversa de WhatsApp. Não vendemos dados, não os partilhamos para publicidade e não os passamos a mais ninguém — salvo se a isso formos legalmente obrigados, por exemplo por ordem de um tribunal ou das autoridades.

O uso do WhatsApp pode implicar transferências de dados para empresas da Meta fora do EEE. Essas transferências ocorrem ao abrigo das garantias da própria Meta, descritas na política de privacidade dela. Se preferir não usar o WhatsApp, escreva-nos no WhatsApp indicado no ponto 1.
